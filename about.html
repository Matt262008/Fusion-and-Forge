Our Story

Built from a Passion for Creating

Hello, I'm Matthew Galvez, founder of Fusion & Form. My passion for engineering and design is what inspired me to create this business. Over the past several years, I have dedicated myself to learning, building, and pushing the limits of what I can create. I've designed everything from miniature robots and AI-controlled robotic arms to go-karts built from scrap parts and even an AI-powered Iron Man helmet featuring a virtual reality heads-up display. Every project has challenged me to grow as an engineer and designer. Through Fusion & Form, my goal is to bring that same creativity, innovation, and attention to detail into every product I make, sharing my passion for engineering with people around the world.

What began as an interest in 3D printing, engineering, and product design grew into a business focused on personalized gifts, home décor, useful accessories, and custom creations.

Every project is an opportunity to solve a problem, improve an idea, or create something meaningful for a customer.
