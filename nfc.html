How It Works

Simple for You. Simple for Your Customers.

NFC lets compatible phones open programmed information when brought close to the tag. A QR code can also be included as a backup option.

01

Choose the Action

Decide what the NFC product should open, such as Wi-Fi, a website, social page, review page, or digital menu.

02

Customize the Design

Choose your logo, colors, wording, stand shape, QR-code placement, and other design details.

03

We Build It

Your product is 3D printed, assembled, programmed, and checked before delivery or pickup.

04

Tap and Connect

Customers tap their compatible phone or scan the QR code to access the programmed destination.
