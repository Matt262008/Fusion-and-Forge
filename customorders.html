How Custom Orders Work

Custom products require a little planning. Here is what happens after you submit your request.

1

Submit Your Idea

Describe your product and provide details about its size, color, quantity, and intended use.

2

Receive a Quote

We review the request and contact you with pricing, estimated completion time, and any questions.

3

Approve and Pay

Once you approve the design and quote, you will receive payment instructions.

4

We Create It

Your product is printed, inspected, finished, packaged, and prepared for pickup or shipping.

Before You Submit

Include as much information as possible. More detail helps us provide a more accurate price and turnaround estimate.

  • Describe what the product should look like.
  • Include approximate dimensions.
  • Choose your preferred color.
  • Tell us how many you need.
  • Mention your deadline, if applicable.
  • Provide a link to reference images when possible.
