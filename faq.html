Printing Information

Materials and Quality

Most products are printed using Elegoo PLA+. PLA+ provides good surface quality, strength, and detail for decorative products, accessories, personalized gifts, and many functional items.
3D-printed products are built in thin layers, so small layer lines may be visible. This is a normal part of the manufacturing process. Products are inspected before delivery, but minor surface variations may still be present.
PLA+ can soften or deform when exposed to high temperatures. Do not leave products inside a hot vehicle, near an oven, directly against a heater, or close to an open flame.
Unless a product is specifically described as food safe, it should not be used for direct food contact. The layered surface of a 3D print can be difficult to clean completely.

Keeping Products in Good Condition

Product Care

Use a soft cloth with cool or lukewarm water. Avoid dishwashers, boiling water, harsh chemicals, rough brushes, and prolonged soaking.
Most PLA+ products are intended for indoor use. Direct sunlight, moisture, freezing conditions, and heat can weaken, fade, or deform the material.
Contact us with a photograph and explanation of what happened. Replacement options depend on the product, when it was purchased, and whether the damage resulted from a printing issue or improper use.
